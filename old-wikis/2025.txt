ultimate survivor IMMUNITY
Survivor War Week will culminate on Friday evening with an event we are calling Ultimate Survivor. While details are not quite finalized, the basic idea is an elimination style tournament where everyone is competing as an individual for significant cash prizes. Throughout the week there will be multiple opportunities to win immunity which will help you advance in Ultimate Survivor. Immunity can be earned three different ways:

Daily Puzzles - Each of the first 5 days of War Week will feature a puzzle taken directly from Survivor. The top performer of each puzzle will earn individual immunity. Daily puzzles do not impact team standings. 

Key Events - Winners of certain events like Settlers of Catan, the Chess Tournament and some yet to be announced contests will earn individual immunity. Like the puzzles, these events do not impact team standings. 

Tribal Immunity Challenge - On Tuesday the three tribes will compete in a team competition with the winner getting team immunity. Every member of the winning tribe automatically advances to the second round of Ultimate Survivor. 

While an individual may win more than one event, no one can have more than one individual immunity. However, members of the winning tribe can also earn an individual immunity meaning they automatically advance two rounds during Ultimate Survivor. See below for additional details.

WAR WEEK FAQs
I have some great pics and videos. Where do I put them?

Reach out to Jet Breuer (@jetbreuer) with any great content you might have. He will know what to do. 

How do I record War Week time in Tense?

Billable project work is recorded the same as always. If you are planning on working extra billable hours, make sure you clear it with your SDM or project lead first. Similarly, learning time is recorded the same as you normally would. If a time entry is not billable or learning but still productive, you can use JG - Admin. For stuff that is nonsense (ex. video games, Beyblades, etc.), use JG  - Team Building. 


When are the hours cut offs this year?

The billable hours cutoff is Thursday at midnight. There are no honor societies or awards for overall hours this year so no cutoff is necessary for those. 


What are the awards at this year's War Week?

Certain individual events will earn immunity for Ultimate Survivor on Friday. In an attempt to make War Week hours more about quality and less about quantity, we will not have the Centurion or Four Score Clubs this year. At closing ceremonies we will recognize: 

Black Midnight - Those who go from Sunday at 12:01 AM until noon (12 hours) with no nonsense. 

War Week MVPs - We are still finalizing MVP categories. Possibly by tribe, possibly overall. 

Billable Hours Champs - It wouldn't be War Week without calling out the people doing the most "real" work. Grind awards for top billers. Remember, check with your SDM before putting up above average hours on your project. 


Are we allowed to have personal guests at War Week? 

Yes and no. Sunday is a great day for guests during Survivor Kids and even Catan. Monday and Wednesday evening should be a little calmer compared to Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. Closing Ceremonies and Open House on Friday night are always a good time for visitors. Each of those is a great time to show off the office to family and friends. On weekdays please limit guests to after hours. We simply don't have the room for extra people and 9 to 5 are primarily intended to be working hours. See Jason for guest requests. 


What about client / prospect guests?  

War Week is an incredible time to show current and potential clients what JG is all about so we do everything we can to accommodate such guests. If you plan to invite clients or prospects, please let Jason and Jessie Z know. If they will be working from JG, coordinate with Ant to make sure we have space reserved for them.
