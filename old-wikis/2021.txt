WAR WEEK FAQS
How do I record War Week time in Tense?

Billable project work and learning time are recorded the same as always. If it does not fit under either of those you can use JG - Admin. Nonsense goes under JG  - Team Building. You should record all JG / War Week related time this week.  

I have some great pics and videos. Where do I put them?

Post your War Week media content to JG Resources > Images > War Week > WW21. Please self select the content you put there. (Don't post 24 photos of the same thing one second apart) 

When are the hours cut offs this year?

The billable hours cutoff (aka The Grind Core Value Award) is Friday at 4 PM to make sure we have time to make it official at Closing Ceremonies. We will also announce anyone who has made Four Score (80) or Centurion (100) as of Closing Ceremonies but please note that non-billable hours count through midnight on Friday. So you might not make an honor society by Closing Ceremonies but the final hours at night could put you over the top. Record all hours though midnight on Friday. 

I am CST / PST, do I have to do the workouts at 6:00 EST?

No. But you must do them 6 am local time and you must check in with Dani or Lucier to note that you are in.
