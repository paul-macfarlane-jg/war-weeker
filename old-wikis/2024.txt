WAR WEEK FAQS
What is different this War Week?

We try to make War Week a little different and a little better each year. Here are a few changes for this edition:

Fewer big meals. Everyone loves the food at War Week but we do go overboard sometimes. We will have breakfast and dinner every day but scale back on lunches. 

Speaking of lunch, everyone's swag pack will include some cash that you can use to go off campus for lunch and hopefully connect with some new JGers. 

Less emphasis on decorations

Two full time resources dedicated to meals, supplies and organization (including Office Mom of the Year, Gina Nichols)

No War Week Kids event. This one kills me but starting the week with a massive party for 40-50 kids adds a lot of stress. 

We will still have an Open House Friday after Closing Ceremonies but no official events that evening. 

The return of one of our greatest events ever, Black Tuesday (more on that later)


The team scoring is much different this year as well. Simply put, the team with the highest level of participation will definitely be in contention for the Battle of the Bands title. Between Spirit Points (see below) and Participation Events (like Midnight Club and Black Tuesday), individuals can easily pile up a large number of points for their teams. 

Where do I find information about War Week?

Pretty much everything you need to know can be found on this page: schedule, meals, events and challenges. You should also follow #war_week_2024 on Slack and check out the JG Calendar where events will be posted as the schedule is finalized. 

How do I record War Week time in Tense?

Billable project work is recorded the same as always. If you are planning on working extra billable hours, make sure you clear it with your SDM first. Similarly, learning time is recorded the same as you normally would. If a time entry is not billable or learning, you can use JG - Admin. Nonsense (ex. Beer Pong tourney) goes under JG  - Team Building. You should record all JG / War Week related time this week.  

I have some great pics and videos. Where do I put them?

Reach out to Jet Breuer (@jetbreuer) with any great content you might have. He will know what to do. 

What are the awards at this year's War Week?

Events with individual winners and will count towards the Battle of the Bands standings. We will also have five formal awards / honor societies each War Week:

Midnight Club - For those who kick off their week on Sunday at 12:01 AM and work straight through until at least noon (12 hours). 

Four Score Club - For those who go over 80 hours total for the week.

Centurion Club - This is now a lifetime honor society. That means that once you make it, you are in for life. You do not (and probably should not) repeat each year. If you are not in, all you need to do to gain entry is go 100 hours total for the week. Piece of cake.  

War Week MVPs - Each genre will elect their own MVP.

Billable Hours Champs - It wouldn't be War Week without calling out the people doing the most "real" work. Grind awards for top billers. Remember, check with your SDM before putting up above average hours on your project. 

When are the hours cut offs this year?

The billable hours cutoff is Thursday at midnight. The over all hours cutoff is Friday at midnight. If you are staying late Friday you can enter projected hours for that evening in advance so they count towards your overall total. 


Are we allowed to have guests at War Week? 

Yes and no. Sunday is a great day for guests as there will be a bunch of games and contests going on. The Variety Show on Thursday should be a great event. Same goes for Closing Ceremonies and Open House on Friday night. Each of those is a great time to show off the office to family and friends. On weekdays please limit guests to after hours. We simply don't have the room for extra people this year and 9 to 5 are primarily intended to be working hours. See Jason for guest requests. 


NOTE: If you are planning on having client guests, please inform Jason and JZ so we can make sure that the appropriate arrangements are made.
