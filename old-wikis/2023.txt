WAR WEEK FAQS
Where do I find information about War Week?

Pretty much everything you need to know can be found on this page: schedule, meals, events and challenges. You should also follow #war_week_2023 on Slack and check out the JG Calendar where events will be posted as the schedule is finalized. 

How do I record War Week time in Tense?

Billable project work is recorded the same as always. If you are planning on working extra billable hours, make sure you clear it with your PM or RM first. Similarly, learning time is record the same as you normally would. If a time entry is not billable or learning, you can use JG - Admin. Nonsense (ex. Beer Pong tourney) goes under JG  - Team Building. You should record all JG / War Week related time this week.  

I have some great pics and videos. Where do I put them?

Reach out to Josh Whelan (@jwhelan) with any great content you might have. He will know what to do. 

What are the awards at this year's War Week?

Each event listed in the sections below will have individual winners and will count towards the House Cup Challenge. We will also have five formal awards / honor societies each War Week:

Midnight Club - For those who kick off their week on Sunday at 12:01 AM and work straight through until at least noon (12 hours). 

Four Score Club - For those who go over 80 hours total for the week.

Centurion Club - This is now a lifetime honor society. That means that once you make it, you are in for life. You do not (and probably should not) repeat each year. If you are not in, all you need to do to gain entry is go 100 hours total for the week. Piece of cake.  

War Week MVPs - Each house will elect their own MVP. Additionally, we will have an overall MVP for all of War Week which will be decided by popular vote. 

Billable Hours Champ - It wouldn't be War Week if we didn't call out the house doing the real work. The house that hits the highest percentage of their average weekly billable hours takes the honors. 

When are the hours cut offs this year?

The billable hours cutoff is Thursday at midnight. The over all hours cutoff is Friday at midnight. If you are staying late Friday you can enter projected hours for that evening in advance so they count towards your overall total. 


Are we allowed to have guests at War Week? 

Yes and no. Sunday is a great day for guests, specifically the War Week Kids event. Closing ceremonies and after hours on Friday are also a great time to bring in friends and family. On weekdays please limit guests to after hours. We simply don't have the room for extra people this year. See Jason for guest requests. 

Is there a checklist of what I need to do to prepare for War Week?

Pretty much everything you need to know is listed below but here are a few key items:

If you will be at JGHQ, you must fill out the War Week Meal Survey. 

If you are remote and traveling in, please coordinate travel plans with Amanda and the HR Department.

Use the links and forms below to sign up for events or reach out to the event coordinator directly.

WAR WEEK 2023 HOUSE CUP SCORING
The winner of the House Cup will be decided by points awarded in the events outlined below plus a series of house competitions. The team with the most points wins. 

*** THE HONOR SYSTEM IS IN EFFECT FOR ALL EVENTS ***
DISCRETIONARY HOUSE POINTS UP FOR GRABS:

📢 Morning Announcements Each house is responsible for the morning announcement covering the meals and events on their assigned day. The format is up to each house.

☕ War Week Mug Shot - Which house can post the best mug shot taken during War Week?

🖼 Collage Wall Submission - Each house can submit one collage wall submission. It can be made by one person or a team of them. Note that the submission does not have to be Harry Potter themed.

🥫 Beer Cans - To assist in eliminating the duplicates from my uncle's vintage beer can collection, I am looking for each house to submit up to five 12 ounce beer cans to add to the display in my office. I will also accept one 16 ounce can if you wish to submit one. Best SET wins. 

🍴 Meals - Each house will be putting on breakfast, lunch and dinner on their assigned day. JG takes its food very seriously so this might be the most important category of all. 

📣 Spirit / Discretionary - Spirit will be judged across several categories: JG gear, HP gear, decorations, participation, enthusiasm and whatever special tricks and gimmicks your house can come up with. 

+1 point to Gryffindor - Jet Breuer lent his astonishing video editing skills to Hufflepuff to make their amazing morning announcement video. While the competition is fun, War Week is about making magic happen for JG as a whole. 

+1 point to Ravenclaw - Jory Hutchins, the defending Catan Champ, pulled himself out of this year's tournament in order to make numbers work for others when we had several no shows

+5 points to Gryffindor - Paul Macfarlane straight up bribed me with a donation to my favorite charity (Which he doubled after the fact. Baller move)

CHALLENGES DECIDED BY HOUSES:

🕛 Midnight Club One midnight club point is awarded to a house for each member that goes from 12:00 am to noon on Sunday (12 hours). You must "sign in" via the posted forms at both the start and end of midnight club. Points are 10 / 5 / 3 / 1 based on midnight club points. 

🏴‍☠️️ Black Tuesday One Black Tuesday point is awarded to a house for each member that goes from 6:00 am to 1:00 pm Tuesday without talking unnecessarily (7 hours). You must "sign in" via the posted forms at both the start and end of midnight club. HQ resources must be in office by 6:00 am. Remote resources are on the honor system. Points are 10 / 5 / 3 / 1 based on Black Tuesday points. 

🏋️🧘‍♀️ Daily Workouts One workout point is awarded to a house for each member that completes all four workouts (2 Beastmode and 2 Yoga). You must "sign in" in the chat for each workout. Points are 10 / 5 / 3 / 1 based on workout points. 

🚶🏽 Stairs Challenge One stairs point is awarded to a house each time a member of that house does at least 5 full trips in one day, Sunday through Thursday. So each person can earn up to 5 stairs points for their house. You must record your stairs as you do them. No sandbagging or backfilling entries. Points are 10 / 5 / 3 / 1 base on stairs points. 

🧙🏼‍♀️ Tri-Wizard Tournament - The last house with someone left wins. Points are 10 / 5 / 3 / 1 based on order of elimination. 

🖼 Battle of the Memes - Points are 10 / 5 / 3 / 1 based on team voting during the talent show. 

💰 Billable Hours - Cut off is midnight on Thursday. Instead of individual contributors, we will be judging billable hours by house based on a running average leading up to War Week. The idea is to make sure we are taking care of our clients and getting everything that needs to be done handled. War Week hours as a team will be compared vs. the four week team average with 10 / 5 / 3 / 1 points being award based on percentage. Note that your house must be at or over their 4 week average to earn any points. Your hours must be in Tense when the report is run Friday morning. 


CHALLENGES DECIDED BY INDIVIDUALS:

🐑 Settlers of Catan - 5 House Cup points to the house of the winner. 3 points to the winner of the second table. 

🧙🏼‍♂️ Magic the Gathering - 3 House Cup points for the advanced champ, 1 House Cup point for the beginner champ 

🃏 War Week Beer & Cards - 3 House Cup points for the advanced champ, 1 House Cup point for the beginner champ

🎮 Gaming Night - John Lee and Kyle Gancarz host tournaments in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, Mario Kart, CS:GO. The winner of each house will earn 3 points for their house

♟ Wizard's Chess Tournament - 5 / 3 / 1 House Cup points for 1st, 2nd & 3rd place. Additionally, 1 point will be awarded to the winner of the beginners bracket.
